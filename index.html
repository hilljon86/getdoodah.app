Color. Draw. Doodah.

A friendly coloring and drawing app for kids on iPad and iPhone. Describe anything, or snap a photo, and Doodah turns it into a coloring page ready for crayons.

A look inside

Color inside the lines, say what to draw, turn a photo into a page, make a card, and keep every page to color again.

A sheet of paper, not an infinite canvas

Every Doodah drawing is one page with edges, just like the paper on the kitchen table. Kids finish a page, feel proud of it, and start the next.

Pages from a description

“A dinosaur having a birthday party.” Say it or type it and get a fresh coloring page.

Pages from a photo

Take a picture of the cat, a toy or the garden and Doodah redraws it as simple, bold line art.

Stay in the lines

Turn it on and color stays inside the shape you started in, so little hands get tidy pages.

Drawn by a grown-up

Sketch a page for your kids to color: a treasure map, a family portrait, a maze.

Cards

Make a folded birthday card, a thank-you note or a get-well-soon, with words on the inside.

Print and share

Print any page in clean black and white, or send it to cousins as a small file they can color in Doodah.

Made for families

No ads and no tracking. Drawings stay in your family’s own iCloud, and purchases sit behind a grown-up gate.

Movies of a drawing

Watch a page being drawn, stroke by stroke, or make the picture move. Save the movie to Photos or send it to family.

Color together

Share a drawing with family through iCloud and color the same page from different homes, or draw together on a FaceTime call.

Support

Stuck, found a bug, or have an idea? Email support@getdoodah.app. We read every message.

Open the page, tap the Share button and choose Print. Doodah prints the page in black and white, sized to fit the paper. Any AirPrint printer on your Wi-Fi will appear in the list.

Tap Share and send the page through Messages, Mail, AirDrop or Files. It travels as a small .colorpage file. If the other person has Doodah, tapping the file opens it straight into their library, ready to color. Without Doodah, they can still print the attached picture.

Yes, on every iPad and iPhone signed in to the same Apple ID with iCloud turned on. Drawings sync through your own private iCloud storage, so a device signed in to a different Apple ID (for example a child's own Apple ID) has its own library.

Each new page uses this month's free pages first, then your Premium allowance, and only then a credit. “Try again” makes a new page, so it uses one too. If something goes wrong and no page is made, nothing is used.

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions, choose Doodah and tap Cancel Subscription. Premium keeps working until the end of the period you've paid for. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Every family gets a few free coloring pages and one free movie each month. Premium adds 60 pages and 5 "Make it move" movies a month, and credit packs top you up and never expire: a page is one credit, a movie is five. Drawing, cards, printing, "Watch it being drawn" movies, sharing with family and drawing your own coloring pages are always free.

Only when you ask for something that needs our server. Making a page from words or a photo sends the description or a black-and-white copy of the photo; "Make it move" sends a picture of the finished drawing. Neither is stored by us. Everything else, including "Watch it being drawn", happens on the device, and drawings sync only through your family's own iCloud.

Open a drawing, tap Share and choose "Share with family". Doodah sends an iCloud invitation; whoever accepts can color the same page from their own iPad or iPhone, and each person can only erase their own marks. "Draw together on FaceTime" does the same live during a call. Stop sharing from the same menu to take the page back.
